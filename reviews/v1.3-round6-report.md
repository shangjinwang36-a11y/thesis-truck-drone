## Every change that touched S

S’s nominal plans are byte-identical to round 5 on all six maps. S’s operators and the iteration schedule were not edited. The shared edits are:

1. **Posterior support.** `unrevealed_failure_probability(50, 0, 10)` is `0.036079545454545454`. After ten successes, `P(K=2)` falls from 0.5 to 0.4432 and the next customer’s failure probability rises from 0.030 to 0.03608. `enumerate_local_world` now sums only over K with positive posterior weight and over failure counts that K can still produce (`b ≤ 2`, and `b = 0` only once two failures are already revealed). These functions are not called by the nominal or recovery search. S and X share them.
2. **Success batches do not open a recovery.** `batch_opens_recovery` is what `simulate.run_episode` consults. A batch whose deliveries all succeed updates history and does not create a budget. A batch that contains a new failure opens one recovery process, with one 1000-evaluation budget, for every failure in that batch. Same-time events are ordered by customer id, then parcel id. S and X use this path.
3. **Episode-state reset.** `reset_planner_episode_state` runs at the start of every episode and clears `_column_pools` and `_scenario_cache` on the algorithm object. A fresh config has neither, so the clear is empty. It is there so a reused object cannot carry another episode’s pool or cache. S and X both call it.
4. **Column-pool key.** The pool key is `column_pool_key(case_id, allow_cross)`, which is `(case_id, allow_cross)`. The hidden failure set is not part of it. S and X share `hybrid._pool`.
5. **Planner view whitelist.** `load_run_config` returns `explicit_planner_whitelist` of the manager view. Generator version, check samples, `M_note`, and strings that name tune or dev seeds are removed. The draft view that the registered hash covers is still produced by `build_resolved_config` and was not edited.

Two S episode costs differ from round 5 under those identical nominal plans: C50 seed 9 is 181.83 yuan (round 5 was 182.27) and RC50 seed 106 is 408.61 yuan (round 5 was 408.37). No other S episode cost moved. The posterior functions are off the recovery path, and the success-batch predicate matches the previous skip. The reset runs on a fresh algorithm object.

## What X gained on top of that

## Why cross wins or loses on each map

**C50 wins.** The new cluster and floor ranking published a one-cross plan 10.32 yuan cheaper than S. Episodes keep +2.22%, and `g_RX` is +1.88% of that, so the gain is the plan rather than the recovery policy. R, which flies S’s plan with X’s recovery, saves only +0.34%. Seed 6 still has no legal completion under this plan, which is why the pair count is 19.

**R50 loses.** The published plan has no cross sortie and is 2.14 yuan more expensive than S. `g_RX` is −2.40%, almost the whole −2.49%. The operator never accepted a cross, and the same-truck plan X published is worse than S’s. Cross proposals that fail the floor are still evaluated when no meeting clears it, and those evaluations come out of the same 1000. On this map that spend did not buy a sortie.

**RC50 loses, by a small amount.** No published cross. The nominal is 0.66 yuan cheaper than S and the episodes are 0.38% more expensive. Seed 4 finishes under S and not under X. Two cross rescues are not enough to move `g_SR` above zero (−0.27%).

**C100 does not convert a cheaper nominal.** Two cross sorties, nominal −3.71 yuan, episodes −0.03%. `g_RX` is −0.47%. R is the cheapest of the three (+0.45% against S). The crosses are in the plan, and the hidden failures do not land where the rescue proxy thought they would. The nominal saving does not survive.

**R100 wins, and it is the only Holm rejection.** One cross, nominal −12.83 yuan, episodes +1.67% (19 cheaper, 1 costlier). `g_RX` is +1.45%. This plan is the round-5 plan; the new operator did not replace it. Seven cross rescues add the remaining +0.22% through R.

**RC100 wins, on a worse nominal than round 5.** The new operator replaced the 672.15 plan with a 675.84 plan, still one cross. Episodes are +0.83% (round 5 was +0.95%) and cross rescues rose from 6 to 8. Rescue-aware J accepted a higher truck cost. The episode gain shrank with it.

## Next fix, not implemented

Two X-only changes, both inside the current loop, neither of them a setting change:

1. If no meeting clears the existing 0.08333 kWh floor on the estimated schedule, return no candidate and do not spend the evaluation. Today the search falls back to the best meeting that only clears `E_min`, spends the evaluation, and on R50 and RC50 publishes no cross and a worse same-truck plan. Skipping that evaluation leaves the count for the same-truck repair inside the same 1000.
2. Accept a cross only when plain v1.3 J improves. Keep the rescue proxy for ranking candidates, not for accepting a higher `C0`. That is the RC100 step from 672.15 to 675.84. C100’s separate failure is that a cheaper cross nominal did not survive the hidden failures; the proxy should be checked against the training scenarios’ realized parcels before it can outrank a same-truck champion.

The budget, the margin, H, and the failure model stay as they are. Raising any of them to chase 5% is not proposed as a code change.
