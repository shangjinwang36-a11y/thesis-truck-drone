# v1.3 round 4

Same branch and the same pull request. Test seeds 4001–4030 were not used. Tune is 101–110. Dev is 1–10. The run is `r6/results/v13_round4`.

## Hard constraints

- [x] **No 0.2 failure weight, for S or for X.** Unchanged from round 3. Under `v13_rules`, planning uses `3/(2N)` and `1/(N(N−1))`. `tests/test_v13_acceptance.py` is 14 passed, including the test that fails if a v1.3 planner reads 0.2.
- [x] **X is one search, not “S first, then X”.** The same-truck loop is not a second algorithm and it does not read S’s result. Cross moves are tried on the plan that loop produced, inside the same 1000 nominal evaluations, and a cross is published only when v1.3 J improves and the plan still contains a cross sortie. Recovery is still one catalog: same-truck entries and cross-truck rescues together, with no stagnation hand-off.
- [x] **No experiment setting was changed.** Rules, failure model, probabilities, budgets (1000/1000, 60 s), seeds, fleet, energy, H, prices, statistics, and the 0.08333 kWh cross margin are unchanged. S’s nominal costs match round 3 on every map (C50 209.87, R50 441.13, RC50 351.59, C100 471.64, R100 740.63, RC100 677.80). No shared bug was found, so S’s operators were not edited.

## Diagnosis of the round-3 same-truck gain

Round 3’s published X plans on C50, RC50, and RC100 contained no cross sortie, and they were much cheaper than S (nominal gaps −39.54, −28.43, −14.94). Those plans are legal for S.

The diagnostic reran seeds 101–110 and 1–10. Completion under S’s recovery was 20/20, 18/20, and 20/20. The saving survives S’s recovery, so it is not an X-only recovery trick. It is the nominal plan.

S does not reach those plans because they are a different point on the same-truck neighbourhood, not a point S’s operators reject. In round 3 the cross operators ran inside the annealing loop. Their evaluations advanced the counter that sets the temperature and that ends each 150-evaluation slice. After the first few cross evaluations the same-truck acceptance decisions diverged, and on C50, RC50, and RC100 the new path happened to finish at a cheaper same-truck plan. On R50 and R100 the same mechanism finished at a worse one. Re-running the slice with the cross evaluations removed makes the recorded same-truck costs line up with S again. That is search-path dependence, not a defect in a move, in evaluation, or in acceptance that S and X share. S was left as it is.

## What changed in X

Cross proposals no longer run inside the same-truck annealing loop, so they cannot move its temperature, its slice length, or its random stream. After that loop has stopped, the evaluations still left inside the 1000 are spent on cross moves: retargeting a same-truck recovery onto another truck when the proxy saving is positive, then a short cross-enabled search on a copy of the same-truck plan. A result replaces the same-truck plan only when v1.3 J is better, the 0.08333 kWh margin holds, and at least one cross sortie remains. A cheaper same-truck plan found on that side path is discarded. Recovery is unchanged apart from a count of how often the chosen recovery truck is not the launch truck.

## Result

Six-map equal-weight `(C_S − C_X) / C_S` is **+0.71%**, 95% CI **[+0.39%, +1.06%]** (B = 10000, seed 20261114, 20 seeds per map). Tune alone is +0.64% [+0.23%, +1.13%]. Dev alone is +0.77% [+0.33%, +1.30%]. Round 3’s +4.84% does not survive once the same-truck artifact is removed. No map has X significantly more expensive. Holm rejects only R100 and RC100, both toward X cheaper.

Nominal true cost, X minus S: C50 0, R50 0, RC50 0, C100 −3.32, R100 −11.12, RC100 −2.36. The three zero gaps are the same plan S found. The three negative gaps are plans that contain cross sorties and pass the margin. S publishes no cross sortie on any map.

Where the gain comes from: on R100 and RC100, most of `g_SX` is `g_RX`, the nominal cross. On C50 and R50 the nominal plans match, and the small gain is `g_SR`, from the cross rescues (2 and 4 uses across 20 episodes). RC50 has neither a nominal cross nor a cross rescue, and `g_SX` is +0.12%. Across all 120 X episodes the recovery chose a cross-truck rendezvous 15 times. That is a real cross-truck contribution, and it is about one percent, not five.

Completion is the same for S and X on C50, R50, RC50, and RC100. C100: X misses one world S finishes (McNemar 1, 0, p = 1). R100: X finishes two worlds S misses (0, 2, p = 0.5). No completion test rejects. The largest recovery process used 996 evaluations. Wall-clock hits: 0. The longest recovery was 9.41 s.

The 5% target on every map was not met. Once the same-truck plan is the one S’s own search reaches, the cross moves that also pass the 0.08333 kWh margin are worth well under 5% of cost on these six maps. The largest nominal gap in this run is R100, −11.12 yuan on a 740.63 plan, which is 1.5%.

## Proposals that were not implemented

- Spend more than the leftover part of the 1000 on the cross search. That would displace same-truck evaluations again. The cap stays 1000.
- Relax the 0.08333 kWh margin. Several cross moves that look cheaper before the margin are illegal under it. The margin stays.
- Change S so that it follows the perturbed round-3 path and finds the 170-yuan C50 plan. That plan is S-feasible, but reaching it requires a different search trajectory. That would be a change to S’s algorithm, which this round does not make.
