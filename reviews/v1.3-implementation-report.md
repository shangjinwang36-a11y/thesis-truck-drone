## What changed

The experiment manager builds `resolved_config` from `package_light.json` and the frozen round-11 S nominal plans (`tdr/v13_config.py`). Canonical SHA-256 of the null-M draft is `18aa0b6b9fded9941174378899dd799b5d78e18cfa12a05d13f813e943c934ae`. The planner whitelist view hashes to `1083a619336d186f36e4133a9616f8b4bb1bf27f2c2dafaedff59f7b63d7f748`. A mismatch refuses to start. Unknown `implementation_id` values raise. M1 or M2 null, or status other than `FROZEN`, refuses a formal run.

Physics and accounting now follow v1.3 for S and X together:

- Hover power stays linear in payload, matching round-11 `hover_power`. The X nominal cross-truck gate is one threshold, `E_min + max(0.05 E_cap, E_hover(90 s, 0 kg))` = 0.083333… kWh. It is not an extra 90 s of waiting and it is not applied to S.
- Every recorded drone energy must stay at or above `E_min` (0.05 kWh). The landed wait/recovery branch raises if it is ever taken. `land_and_wait` and `soft_h` stay off, including in contingency pricing. A negative hover duration is infeasible, not an energy credit.
- Swap-only. On-truck charge energy and its cost stay 0. Electricity is `0.731012/0.9` times the AC energy needed to refill used batteries. A swap debits the carrier that is executing it, once. An in-progress swap is reserved at the cut and is not debited again when it completes. Cloning a state does not refill the spares.
- A parcel already revealed as an air failure cannot remain on `sortie.air` and be priced as a successful delivery.
- Handover placement clears any earlier ground copy of that parcel before inserting the single delivery after recovery.
- Legacy driver event `W` is mapped to `SWAP` in one place.
- Hidden failures are `K ∈ {1, 2}` with equal probability, drawn without replacement from all air-eligible customers. The registered check samples for seeds 1–3 match.
- Planning uses `J_hat = (1/(2 M1)) Σ C(single) + (1/(2 M2)) Σ C(pair)` on one scenario set shared by S and X. The scenario RNG is `SHA256(["tdr-plan-scenarios-v1.1", case, base_algorithm_seed])` and does not read an environment seed. A scenario with no legal completion does not become 0 or a 400 yuan penalty; that comparison has no finite J.
- Recovery uses `P(K=2 | f=1, s) = 2(N−s−1)/(3N−2s−3)` and the binomial posterior. The Bernoulli sensitivity posterior is a separate function. After two revealed failures the remaining failure probability is 0.
- Observations at the same simulated timestamp are one recovery process. A gap of 0.0005 s is two processes. `tolerance_time_s` is not used to merge them.
- Each recovery process shares one cap of 1000 full evaluations and 60 s of wall clock across initialisation, the catalog, MILP checks, the main search, scenario evaluations and contingency pricing. The old top-ups (`+60`, `+120`, `+24`, rescue) are gone. Sub-budget exhaustion is not reported as a full-cap exhaustion. The 60 s cap is armed; this run did not hit it.
- X does not read an independent S search (`adopt_from`, `x_s_second_start`, `x_adopt_s_nominal`, `x_adopt_s_if_cross`). R publishes S's nominal plan and spends 0 nominal evaluations, then recovers with X's permission. S recovers only onto the sortie's launch truck. A new ordinary sortie returns to the truck that launches it.
- `x_recovery_wider` is read. The same Boolean and the same MILP `top_k` are used for S and X. Only the cross-truck neighbourhood itself is X-only.
- Planner logs drop `hidden_failure_parcels`, `air_hidden` and the environment seed. Cache keys use the public history, spares, strategy, probability model, recovery version and the training scenario set. They do not contain the true failure set. Posterior functions do not accept a `HiddenWorld`.
- Stopped episodes are priced only when a legal contingency finish exists under hard H and hard `E_min`. Otherwise the cost is missing. Statuses are `COMPLETED_ONLINE`, `COMPLETED_CONTINGENCY`, `NO_LEGAL_COMPLETION_FOUND` and `HARD_CONSTRAINT_VIOLATION`.

## M1 and M2

Chosen on tune material only: `M1 = 10`, `M2 = 10`. Dev seeds 1–10 were not used for the choice, and neither were 2001–2010, 1001–1010, 3001–3030 or 4001–4030.

Procedure (`r6/choose_m.py`, output `r6/results/v13_m_selection.json`):

1. Candidate plans are the published round-11 S and X nominals. They are fixed plans, not a warm start for the new search.
2. Cost of a failure set is one full evaluation of a deterministic ground recourse (failed air orders moved to the legal truck, then `evaluate_plan` under H). This is a sizing policy, not the episode ALNS.
3. Scenarios come from the isolated planning RNG at `base_algorithm_seed`, 40 singles and 40 pairs. Nested prefixes give M = 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40.
4. The smallest M where the six-map ranking of `J_hat` matches the M=40 ranking, and the worst relative standard error is at most 2%, is M=10. Worst relative SE at M=10 is 0.57%. M=8 still flips R50.
5. Tune seeds 101–110 are stored as a realised-cost check under the same recourse policy. They are not the sample that picked M.

`M1+M2 = 20`, so a nominal search can score on the order of fifty candidates inside the 1000-evaluation cap. The active draft hash, after writing M1=M2=10 and the note that they were chosen on tune seeds, is `f41373f995e4b0da84f159aeb7d152ce0099d023bbb3e682e23c74f92709172f`. The null-M draft hash is unchanged and is what startup still checks.

Some recourse scenarios on R and RC maps have no legal completion. Those have no finite cost. The sizing study left them out of `J_hat` rather than inserting 0 or 400. The search does not average the finite scenarios and still call the result J: one missing scenario makes that comparison's J undefined.

## Rule ambiguities and how they were resolved

1. **Successes and the per-customer failure rate.** The formula `P = Σ w_k (k−f)/(N−f−s)` is implemented exactly. For f=0 the expected number of remaining failures falls as successes arrive, but the per-customer rate can rise because the pool shrinks faster than that expectation. The parenthetical “成功观察会降低剩余失败概率” is read as the drop in the expected count, not as a second formula. N=50, f=0, s=10 gives expected remaining failures 1.443 against a prior 1.5, and a per-customer rate 0.0361 against the prior 0.030.

2. **Training-scenario draws.** Each of the M1 singletons and M2 pairs is an independent uniform draw. Repeats across scenarios are allowed. The alternative, a without-replacement design of the collection of subsets, is not what the generator sentence says.

3. **Phase switch inside the 1000.** Once a feasible recovery exists and 100 evaluations have been spent, the rest of the same cap goes to the main search. If nothing is feasible, search continues past 100 until the cap or the candidate list is exhausted. The old behaviour stopped at 100 and left the rest unused. The last 4 slots of the same 1000 are held so contingency pricing can run; they are not an extra allowance.

4. **`x_recovery_wider`.** Round 11 set the flag on X and the catalog ignored it for S, and one fallback catalog did not receive it at all. It is now read for both strategies with the same value. The baseline turns it on for both. The screen is still the existing proxy (`est >= 1e4`), which is not a proof of energy infeasibility; it is no longer an X-only switch.

5. **Scenario cost for choosing M.** Sizing M with the episode ALNS would nest a 1000-evaluation search inside every scenario and could not fit in the nominal cap. M was sized on a deterministic recourse evaluation of fixed plans. Infeasible scenarios were omitted from that variance estimate. The search path does not omit them silently: it refuses a finite J.

6. **Python version of the generator check.** The registered samples and the draft hash were produced under Python 3.13.5. This machine is Python 3.12.3. The samples reproduce exactly, so the draft hash is unchanged. Result files should be read as run on 3.12.3; the config field `generator_check_samples.python` stays `3.13.5` because that is part of the registered document.

7. **Exact timestamps.** Arrivals are batched only when the simulated timestamp is identical. Rounding to 0.001 s is not used as a merge rule.

8. **Section 9's 4.5% gate.** It was not used to relax a constraint or to retune H. The measured equal-weight saving on these seeds is negative and the confidence interval does not contain 0. That is the result under the corrected rules.

H was not edited. S was not given a weaker energy model, a smaller cap, or a worse constructor than X.
