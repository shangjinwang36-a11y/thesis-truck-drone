## Hard constraints

- [x] **No 0.2 failure weight, for S or for X.** Planning uses `3/(2N)` and `1/(N(N−1))`. The acceptance test fails if a v1.3 planner reads 0.2 or `scenario_p`. `weight_reaction = 0.2` is the ALNS operator-weight reaction, the same constant S already uses, and it is not a failure weight.
- [x] **X is one integrated search.** The cross choice is a roulette step inside the same loop. It is not a tail on leftover evaluations.
- [x] **No experiment setting was changed.** Rules, failure model, probabilities, budgets (1000 / 1000 / 60 s), seeds, fleet, energy, H, prices, statistics, and the 0.08333 kWh margin are unchanged.
- [x] **S’s nominal plans are byte-identical to round 6.** All six `nominal_plan.json` files match. S’s destroy operators, repair operators, scores, and roulette are the same update as round 6.
- [x] **Equal treatment.** The new cross weights exist only when `nominal_cross` is true. S never creates them. The destroy/repair update S and X share was not edited.

## What changed for X

Inside the same loop:

- A meeting that does not clear the 0.08333 kWh floor on the cheap energy check is not returned, and the iteration spends no evaluation. The check is flight plus hover at the payload against `E_cap − floor`.
- A cross is accepted only when plain v1.3 J improves. The rescue proxy is no longer the acceptance test. Ranking inside the operator still adds `3/(2N)` times the failure cost when a second truck can meet the customer inside the same floor, so a placement that leaves a cross rescue open is preferred among legal meetings. The critical-truck hand-off still moves up to two positive-detour customers off the truck that is longest, or already past H.
- Operator weights use the same reaction (0.2), the same 25-iteration segment, the same scores (33 / 9 / 3 / 0), and the same minimum weight (0.1) as S’s destroy and repair weights. X’s actions are `SKIP`, `MULTI`, `RELIEVE`, and `RETARGET`. `SKIP` receives the same-truck reward for that iteration. A cross kind that finds nothing legal, or whose evaluated plan does not improve plain J, scores 0. The roulette uses its own random stream, reseeded each iteration, so it does not move S’s or X’s destroy/repair draws.

## What bounds the cross gain

**The margin removes a slice, and the scheduler removes another.** On R50, 26 of 60 E_min meetings fail the floor, and 15 of the 18 floor-legal meetings that were evaluated are infeasible on the real schedule. The cheap check is necessary and it is optimistic: a meeting can clear the floor estimate and still fail waiting, slots, or the timed energy account. Those 18 evaluations are the crowd-out that remains. Twenty-one other attempts spent nothing.

**Plain J is the acceptance bound.** RC50 is the map this round moved. Six evaluated plans improved plain J, and the published plan has one cross and is 3.07 yuan cheaper than S. Episodes keep +0.17% (`g_RX` +0.45%). Round 6 on this map was −0.38% with no cross. C100 still has two crosses and a nominal 3.71 yuan cheaper than S, and the episodes do not keep it (`g_RX` −0.42%). The failure term in J and the hidden failures are different sets.

**R50 is still the map where cross offers the published plan nothing.** The search accepted one plain-J improvement and then published the same-truck plan, which is the round-6 plan, 2.14 yuan worse than S, with no cross sortie. `g_SX` is −2.50%. SKIP’s weight rose to 3.18 and RELIEVE and RETARGET fell to 0.64, while MULTI rose to 5.92 because of that one in-search accept. The same-truck champion did not move onto S’s plan. The goal that X be no worse than S on a map whose published plan has no cross is not met on R50.

**R100 remains the only significant win,** +1.67%, one cross, and it is the round-6 plan. C50’s +2.22% is the same plan as round 6. RC100’s +0.82% is the same plan as round 6; plain-J acceptance did not replace it with a more expensive nominal.

The bound is therefore three layers: the 0.08333 kWh floor removes up to 43% of geometrically legal meetings, the scheduler rejects most of what the floor estimate lets through on the map where cross is scarce, and plain J plus the hidden failures keep a cheaper nominal from becoming an episode gain except on C50, R100, RC100, and the small RC50 step.
