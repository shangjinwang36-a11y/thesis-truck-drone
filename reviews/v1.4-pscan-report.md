## Model

Each air-eligible parcel fails independently with probability p and stays hidden until an air attempt. The reveal call is the same one the K∈{1,2} world uses. Because failures are independent, the posterior failure probability of a still-unrevealed parcel is p.

The planning objective, for S and for X, is the exact second-order expectation under that distribution:

`J = C0 + p Σ Q_i + p² Σ I_ij`

`Q_i` and `I_ij` are the same recourse increments as v1.3. The scenario cache, when it is filled, draws independent Bernoulli sets on the shared planner stream (salt `tdr-plan-scenarios-bernoulli-v1.4`), M1+M2 draws, same draws for S and X. Acceptance uses the closed form, not a different weight.

## Draft SCENARIO_RULES v1.4 section

Status: **DRAFT, NOT FROZEN.** Do not use test seeds 4001–4030 to choose or confirm p.

**Failure model `bernoulli_p` (optional).** Each air-eligible parcel fails independently with probability p. The failure is hidden until an air delivery is attempted. The observation record is the existing air-arrival observation. A parcel that is never flown is never revealed. The posterior failure probability of any still-unrevealed air-eligible parcel is p, and it does not depend on how many other parcels have failed or succeeded.

**Planning objective.** S and X both minimise

`J = C0 + p Σ_i Q_i + p² Σ_{i<j} I_ij`

with the same recourse increments `Q_i = C_i − C0` and `I_ij = C_ij − C_i − C_j + C0` as v1.3. p is the failure probability. There is no separate planning weight. There is no constant 0.2 unless the chosen p is 0.2. Scenario draws used to fill the shared cache are i.i.d. Bernoulli(p) subsets, M1+M2 of them, from a stream that does not depend on the strategy or on the environment seed.

**Default remains v1.3.** If `failure_model` is unset, the hidden count is still uniform on K∈{1,2} and the weights are still `3/(2N)` and `1/(N(N−1))`.

**Unchanged.** H (hard; the per-map constants from the round-11 S plans are the comparison values, not retuned). `E_min = 0.05` kWh. Nominal cross margin `E_endR ≥ E_min + max(0.05 E_cap, E_hover(90 s, 0 kg))` = 0.08333 kWh on the light package. Budgets 1000 nominal evaluations, 1000 per recovery process, 60 s. Fleet, energy, prices. Statistics: stratified paired percentile bootstrap B = 10000, `numpy.random.default_rng(20261114)`, Holm on the six per-map sign tests, exact McNemar. S’s operators. No warm start of X from S.
