# v1.3 round 2: why X lost, and what changed

Same branch as the v1.3 implementation. Rules, budgets, H, and the hard constraints are unchanged. Seeds are tune 101–110 and dev 1–10 only. Test seeds 4001–4030 were not used. S was not given a smaller cap, a weaker energy model, or a narrower neighbourhood.

The round-1 baseline is `r6/results/v13_baseline`. This round’s run is `r6/results/v13_round2b`.

## Diagnosis

The −3.69% was not one failure. On the shared S nominal, X recovery was usually a tie with S recovery. The loss was the X nominal.

### Same nominal, two recoveries (S versus R)

R publishes S’s nominal plan and recovers with X’s permission. Round 1, over 120 worlds:

| Map | R more expensive than S | R cheaper | tie | mean R−S (yuan) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| C50 | 4 | 3 | 13 | −0.26 |
| R50 | 0 | 1 | 19 | −1.17 |
| RC50 | 2 | 2 | 16 | −2.65 |
| C100 | 4 | 7 | 9 | −2.28 |
| R100 | 1 | 7 | 12 | −0.08 |
| RC100 | 3 | 2 | 15 | +1.09 |

So X recovery did not systematically lose to S recovery. The mean was slightly in R’s favour except on RC100. The episodes where R was worse were real, though, and the containment argument says they should not happen.

What the code was doing:

- The recovery catalog emits launch-truck entries first, up to `guard_n ≈ 498`. The search then hands over to ALNS after 100 evaluations once any feasible plan exists. A new cross-truck recovery point is never evaluated. `cross_ops` only rewrites a recovery that is already cross-truck, so it returns immediately.
- X’s candidate builder appends an extra “drop the broken chain and ground the parcels” plan that S does not have (`_finish_attempts`). When the ordinary repairs are infeasible, that extra plan consumes an evaluation and can be kept. S spends the same evaluations further down the same-truck list. That is why a minority of worlds diverged, sometimes by 10–15 yuan against X and sometimes by 30–40 yuan in X’s favour. It is budget and ordering, not the 0.083 kWh nominal margin (that gate is not applied in recovery).

The label “R→X is negative because X recovery is worse on the same plan” does not match the decomposition. R→X compares S’s nominal with X’s nominal, both recovered under X. S→R is the same-plan recovery comparison, and it was the smaller term.

### X nominal versus S nominal

Round-1 nominal true cost, X minus S: C50 +11.09, R50 +4.81, RC50 +26.93, C100 +7.01, R100 +35.68, RC100 −7.05. Five of six maps, and RC50 in particular, are this gap. It is most of the −3.69%.

Three mechanisms, measured on the round-1 nominal audits:

1. **Acceptance used p = 0.2, not 3/(2N).** `expected_accept` calls `expected_j` with `failure_prob()`. That function prefers `scenario_p`, whose default is 0.2. The main-experiment weight is 3/(2N), which is 0.030 on 50 customers and 0.015 on 100. A cross that raises the true nominal cost by 10 yuan is accepted when the crude recourse term falls by 50 yuan. At the legal weight the same trade needs a recourse fall of about 300–700 yuan. `scenario_on` was false in the single-phase nominal search, so the M1/M2 estimator was not the acceptance test. A periodic `scenario_score` call did run, burned evaluations, and discarded the number (`EXPECTED.true_reeval`).

2. **Crosses were proposed from the first iteration** (`x_cross_from_start`), inside the same 1000 as the same-truck search. On RC50 the funnel was 60 cross proposals, 20 killed by the X nominal energy margin, 18 killed in scheduling, 1 accepted. The margin is the legal 0.08333 kWh gate. It removed proposals. It did not by itself choose the expensive plan. The p = 0.2 test did.

3. **The same-truck search and the cross search were not separated.** X never had a same-truck champion to fall back to. A cross that won the biased test replaced the plan permanently.

S’s nominal search did not read any of the new X-only controls. Re-running S on the unchanged same-truck procedure reproduced the round-1 nominal costs exactly (C50 192.666, and the same match on the other five maps).

## What changed

S’s nominal procedure, cap, and recovery loop are the ones from round 1. Two X-only changes sit inside X’s own budgets.

**Nominal.** X first runs the same same-truck search S runs, with the same constructor and the same 1000-evaluation cap. It does not read S’s result. It runs the procedure itself. Cross-truck moves are tried only with the evaluations that search did not spend, and only while a temporary weight `v13_p = 3/(2N)` is installed. A cross plan is published only when that J improves and the true nominal cost does not rise. Otherwise the same-truck plan is published. The 0.08333 kWh gate still applies to any cross that is published.

**Recovery.** X runs the same-truck recovery on the shared 1000. If 40 iterations pass with no new best, the rest of that same cap is spent on cross-truck catalog entries. A cross plan replaces the same-truck champion only when its true cost is strictly lower. S never sets the stagnation stop, so S still spends the whole cap on same-truck search. The 60 s wall and the 1000 cap are unchanged. The finish reserve of 4 evaluations is unchanged.

## Result

Six-map equal-weight `(C_S − C_X) / C_S` is **+0.262%**. The 95% bootstrap interval is **[−0.063%, +0.589%]** (B = 10000, seed 20261114). Round 1 was −3.693% with interval [−4.652%, −2.706%]. The loss is gone. The interval contains 0, so this is not a significant saving.

Every map has 20 worlds and 20 S–X pairs. Full completion is 1.00 for S, R, and X on every map, and the online rate is the same. No contingency finishes and no stops. The largest recovery process used 996 evaluations. Wall-clock hits: 0. The longest recovery process was 8.13 s, on RC100.

## What was not changed

H, `E_min`, the landed ban, swap accounting, the 1000 / 60 s caps, the failure generator, and M1 = M2 = 10. The p = 0.2 weight is still what S’s nominal acceptance uses, because replacing it is a shared objective change and a trial of it raised C50’s S nominal from 192.67 to 199.81. The legal weight is used only inside X’s cross phase. That is an X-only acceptance rule on an X-only neighbourhood, which is the split the rules allow.
