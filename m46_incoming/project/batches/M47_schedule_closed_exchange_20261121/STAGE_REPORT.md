# M47 schedule-closed detour cross

M46 stays a DROP. This stage does not replace it.

## Mechanism

M46 rebuilt the whole air pool from a prefix-delta clock. Fourteen direct proposals were paid; two were physically feasible and both lost original J; the three direct crosses were not physically feasible. A separate paid check on a recovery swap, using the parent schedule's launch endings and visit arrivals, is physically feasible on the sealed C100 and R100 champions (air set unchanged, two real crosses) and loses original J by about 13 to 46 yuan. Those swaps are not generated.

The operator that is in the search deletes one stop that exists only to recover a terminal sortie and to ground-deliver one parcel. That parcel joins the sortie, the sortie recovers at another truck's existing visit, and the stop is removed. Hover uses the paid parent timeline. A plan is handed to the original physical slot only when the original J proxy improves: geometric detour, depot energy, paid-timeline hover and truck wait, and the same Q and Qij terms. S does not enter. Air cannot shrink. The physical, J, fixed-scenario, hard H, robust and CAS gates are unchanged.
